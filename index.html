Frequently asked questions

How quickly can we onboard and start sending payments?

Most businesses complete automated KYB verification in under 10 minutes and can issue virtual cards or send domestic and international wires immediately.

How are funds safeguarded and insured?

Client funds are held in segregated tier-1 partner bank custodial accounts with pass-through FDIC insurance coverage up to $2.5M via our sweep network.

Does NovaPay integrate with QuickBooks, NetSuite, and Xero?

Yes. Two-way real-time ledger syncing with QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite, and Sage Intacct is built in with automated receipt matching.
